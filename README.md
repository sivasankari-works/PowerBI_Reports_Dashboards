# Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing sales performance across segments, regions, states and product categories.

## Objective
Give a clear view of what drives sales and profit, and where the business can improve.

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | 2.30M |
| Total Profit | 286.40K (~12.4% margin) |
| Total Quantity Sold | 38K |
| Total Discount | 1.56K |

## Dashboard Visuals
- Sales by Segment (pie): Consumer contributes about 50.6% of sales, followed by Corporate (30.7%) and Home Office (18.7%).
- Sales by Order Date (line):Daily sales trend from 2019 to 2021, showing spikes and seasonality.
- KPI cards:Sales, Profit, Quantity and Discount at a glance.
- Sales by Sub-Category (funnel): Ranks sub-categories from Phones and Chairs at the top down to Fasteners at the bottom.
- Sales by State (map): Geographic spread of sales across the US.
- Sales by Region (donut): West leads (31.6%), followed by East (29.6%), Central (21.8%) and South (17.0%).

## Key Insights
- The Consumer segment generates over half of total sales.
- The West and East regions together account for over 60% of sales.
- Phones, Chairs and Storage are among the top-selling sub-categories, while Fasteners, Labels and Envelopes sell least.
- Sales show recurring peaks each year.
